KEY FINDINGS

  • Most of the money is still visible. About 82% of the stolen value sits in wallets the attacker controls, mostly ETH plus the 203.79 BTC that have never moved. Another 10% is frozen.
  • TRON USDT left TRON within hours via THORChain, the USDT0 bridge, Relay, Chainflip, Bitget, HiFiSwap and NEAR Intents, and became ETH. Before that, $23M was cycled through USDD, which Tether cannot freeze.
  • Tornado Cash → Zcash → USDC round trip. … has been deposited into Tornado so far, and deposits were still happening on 13 Oct. Of that, … (…) was traced back out. Most was swapped into shielded Zcash, and about $11M then came back as USDC/ETH through NEAR Intents (timing and amount match).
  • "Custodial" swap services are traceable. Orders at HiFiSwap, Bitget, SWFT and Aeroswap match payouts 1:1 within seconds to minutes, at a constant exchange rate.
  • Cash-out has begun. Deposits went to HitBTC, ChangeHero and Binance, and about $2.6M went to OTC-broker-like wallets on TRON.
  • The earliest attacker action was at 03:55 UTC: a permission lock-out key on a TRON victim wallet, about an hour before the time previously reported.

LEADS FOR FREEZING & ATTRIBUTION

  • Circle: 2.38M USDC in two attacker wallets that has not been frozen: 0x4600…2a6a and 0x3635…ee17.
  • Tether: 1.35M USDT that has not been frozen, at TMAXFF…BLicYy.
  • NEAR Intents: 800k USDT from two failed swaps was never refunded. Its refund address is the attacker's own wallet.
  • Binance: an established deposit address, in use since March 2026, received 27 ETH: 0x5D77…B211. This is the strongest identity lead.
  • Bybit: a withdrawal on 24 Sep funded the attacker's TRON control wallet (tx b284a254…).
  • HitBTC, ChangeHero, Bitget, SWFT, Aeroswap, HiFiSwap, Relay, Chainflip: their account and order records cover the deposits shown in the map. All exchanges should flag the BTC wallets and the Ethereum holding wallets.

If you are a victim: report to local police and to Ledger, and beware of "fund recovery" offers. They are a common follow-up scam.

METHOD & CAVEATS

  • Data. Public explorers and node APIs only: mempool.space, TronGrid, Blockscout, and public RPC nodes for Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon and Solana. Cross-chain swaps were resolved through the public status APIs of Relay, NEAR Intents and LI.FI. THORChain swaps were resolved from the memos written on-chain.
  • Line styles. A solid line is either a transfer between wallets on the trail or a swap resolved from a service's own record (API or memo). A dashed line is a high-confidence match: a payout matched to its deposit by timing and a constant exchange rate (HiFiSwap, Bitget, SWFT, Aeroswap, Chainflip), a Tornado withdrawal attributed by the demixing heuristics, or a NEAR payout from the Zcash round trip. A dotted line is a medium match.
  • Mixers. Tornado deposits cannot be mathematically linked to their withdrawals. A withdrawal counts as confirmed only when it later merged into known attacker wallets. The high-confidence ones combine the excess pool flow over a baseline week, withdrawal bursts to fresh wallets, shared gas sources, and identical onward swaps to Zcash.
  • Victims' privacy. Victim wallet addresses and their transaction hashes are deliberately not published. Victims appear only as totals per chain, and so do unidentified senders, who may also be victims.
  • Values. USD figures are estimates at the time of each transfer, and balances "now" come from an automated wallet watch. Native BNB/POL movements are not covered by the keyless sources, a gap of about 59 BNB.
